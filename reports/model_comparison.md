# Model comparison

Nested, stratified 5-fold cross-validation, repeated 3 times (1,000 applicants). Cost = 5 × approved bad + 1 × rejected good, per application; the threshold is tuned inside each training fold. Generated by `scripts/evaluate_model.py`.

|                                                           | ROC-AUC       |   Cost per application | Approval rate   | Bad rate among approved   |
|:----------------------------------------------------------|:--------------|-----------------------:|:----------------|:--------------------------|
| Logistic regression (baseline)                            | 0.781 ± 0.032 |                  0.548 | 33.6%           | 8.3%                      |
| XGBoost, no monotone constraints                          | 0.797 ± 0.031 |                  0.563 | 38.5%           | 10.4%                     |
| XGBoost, incl. protected attributes                       | 0.796 ± 0.033 |                  0.548 | 39.0%           | 9.8%                      |
| XGBoost, monotone, protected excluded (dashboard default) | 0.796 ± 0.030 |                  0.564 | 38.2%           | 10.4%                     |
| Approve everyone (no model)                               | –             |                  1.5   | 100.0%          | 30.0%                     |
